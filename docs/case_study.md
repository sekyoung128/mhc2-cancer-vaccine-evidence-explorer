# Case studies: experimental purpose and assay evidence

These examples draw on recorded machine-assisted literature assessment and documented researcher-approved decisions. They do not attribute every retrieval or analysis step to independent human review. Native vimentin is one of the six original PRIMARY role corrections; PSA is a later resolution of an initially unresolved pair. No copyrighted figures or substantial passages are reproduced.

## Native vimentin — PRIMARY role correction

**PMID 32086539; IEDB epitope 2238477.**

### Initial problem or machine classification

The machine assigned `include/cancer_target` from source/title context. That did not distinguish the native peptide from the phosphorylated vimentin targets studied in the paper.

### Primary-literature evidence

The synthetic-peptide Methods and Figure 5 identify native unphosphorylated vimentin as a comparison against phospho-vimentin targets. The represented native-peptide-stimulated colorectal-cancer PBMC IFN-gamma observations are negative. Figure 2 phosphopeptide-primed healthy CD4-line findings are separate context, not the priming experiment for the current native-peptide assay.

Primary source: [PMID 32086539](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32086539/), synthetic-peptide Methods, Figure 5 and corresponding CRC PBMC Results; Figure 2 contextual specificity.

### Final documented interpretation

PRIMARY MV01 assessment proposed, and the canonical researcher-approved record documents, `include/relevant_comparator`. This is a biological-role correction, not an inclusion-status or assay-outcome correction. The decision follows the native peptide's experimental comparison role; negative response alone does not prove comparator identity.

### Scientific significance

A peptide can be relevant to cancer-vaccine interpretation without being the intended tumour target. Distinguishing native and modified reagents prevents responses to one from being assigned to the other.

### Remaining uncertainty

Positive phosphopeptide responses, DR53/DR14 findings and tumour recognition must not transfer to the native peptide. Positive native allele-specific response and endogenous recognition remain unestablished. The distributed pair note preserves this attribution boundary.

## PSA — post-validation resolution with Negative records preserved

**PMID 15837732; IEDB epitope 1948755.**

### Initial problem or machine classification

The frozen pair remained unresolved. A later machine-assisted primary-source audit proposed inclusion of the shorter PSA target determinant; it was not one of the six original role corrections.

### Primary-literature evidence

Figure 1B identifies the exact PSA221–233 peptide. Figure 8B–C and accompanying Results report responsiveness of a CD4 line established with the longer PSA221–240 peptide. IEDB assays **18141060 and 18141061** are nevertheless recorded as **Negative**. Both values remain unchanged; the paper/export discrepancy is documented rather than resolved by rewriting measurements.

Primary source: [PMID 15837732](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15837732/), Figure 1B, Figure 8B–C and minimal-epitope Results. The distributed note retains PDF hash and page references; no PDF is distributed.

### Final documented interpretation

Following the evidence-supported proposal, documented researcher sign-off approved `include/cancer_target` in the separate post-validation layer. The peptide is an intended shorter PSA target determinant. This role adjudication did not correct IEDB measurements or retroactively improve the frozen PRIMARY result.

### Scientific significance

Biological target role and experimental response are separate questions. An explicit source discrepancy can coexist with a defensible role decision and must remain visible to downstream users.

### Remaining uncertainty

The 13-mer is a rechallenge reagent, not independently established as the original priming reagent. Figure 8 uses autologous PBMC antigen-presenting cells; exclusive single-allele proof cannot be borrowed from the parent's engineered-cell experiments. Native intact 13-mer display, tumour killing and vaccine efficacy are not established. Portfolio edits minimise subject linkage while preserving these limitations.
